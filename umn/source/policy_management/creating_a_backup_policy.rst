Creating a Backup Policy
========================

A backup policy allows CBR to automatically back up vaults at specified times or intervals. Periodically generated backups ensure quick data restoration in the event of corruption or loss.

You can use the default backup policy or create a custom one to enable periodic data backups for resources.

The backup service experiences peak usage from 22:00 to 08:00, during which delays may occur. To ensure optimal performance, it is recommended that you evaluate your service types and stagger backups across discrete time periods.

Constraints
-----------

-  A backup policy must be enabled before it can be used for periodic backups.
-  A user can create a maximum of 32 backup policies.
-  When expired backups are deleted, automatic backups will be deleted, but manual backups will not.
-  Only servers in the **Running** or **Stopped** state and disks in the **Available** or **In-use** state can be backed up.
-  CBR by default performs a full backup for a resource in the initial backup and incremental backups in subsequent backups.
-  The minimum interval between two full backups is one day.
-  In CloudPond scenarios, backups in vaults are retained only by quantity. A maximum of seven backups can be retained.

Procedure
---------

#. Log in to the CBR console.

   a. In the upper left corner, click |image1| and select a region.
   b. Click |image2| and choose **Storage** > **Cloud Backup and Recovery**.

#. Choose **Policies** in the navigation pane and click the **Backup Policies** tab. In the upper right corner, click **Create Policy**.

#. Click **OK**. After the backup policy is created, you can view it in the backup policy list.

#. Locate the desired vault and choose **More** > **Apply Backup Policy** in the **Operation** column to apply the policy to the vault. You can then view the applied policy on the vault details page, and data will be periodically backed up to the vault based on the policy.

Example
-------

At 10:00 a.m. on Monday, a user sets a backup policy for their vault to instruct CBR to execute a backup task at 02:00 a.m. every day and retain a maximum of three backups. As of 11:00 a.m. on Saturday, three backups will be retained, which are generated on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday. The backups generated at 02:00 a.m. on Tuesday and Wednesday have been automatically deleted.
